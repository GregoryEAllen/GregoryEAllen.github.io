I am a Senior Engineering Scientist in the Enabling Capabilities Division (ECD) of the 
[Advanced Technology Laboratory](https://wwwext.arlut.utexas.edu/atl.shtml) (ATL) at
[Applied Research Laboratories](https://www.arlut.utexas.edu/),
[The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu/) (ARL:UT).
Our laboratory is located in north Austin on UT's
[Pickle Research Campus](https://www.utexas.edu/research/off-campus-research-sites) (PRC).
I have previously been a member of the Special Warfare Division (SWD) and the Sonar Development Division (SDD), also in ATL. I first came to ARL in 1986 as a member of the High School Apprentice Program.
